Our Story

Rooted in Tradition,
Baked with Heart

Founded in 2005, Golden Crumb is a family-owned bakery built on a simple belief — that great bread doesn't need shortcuts. Every loaf, every pastry, every cookie is made by hand using natural ingredients and recipes passed down through generations.

We bake fresh every single morning, starting at 4am, so that when you walk through our doors the smell of warm bread fills the air. We believe baking is an act of love, and we put that love into everything we make.
